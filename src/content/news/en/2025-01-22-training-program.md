## Empowering Communities Through Education

RomaNET is launching a comprehensive training program designed to equip Roma community members, educators, and civil society organizations with the tools and knowledge to identify, document, and respond to hate speech effectively.

## Training Modules

Our program includes:
- **Hate Speech Recognition**: Learn to identify different forms of online and offline hate speech
- **Documentation Techniques**: Best practices for safely documenting incidents
- **Digital Safety**: Protecting yourself and your community online
- **Counter-Narrative Creation**: Developing positive messages that challenge stereotypes
- **Legal Rights**: Understanding your rights and available legal remedies

## Who Should Attend

These training sessions are designed for:
- Roma community members and leaders
- Educators and youth workers
- Civil society organizations
- Journalists and media professionals
- Anyone committed to fighting discrimination

## Upcoming Sessions

Training sessions will be held in:
- **Spain** - February 2025
- **Italy** - March 2025
- **Greece** - April 2025

All sessions will be available in local languages with materials provided in English, Spanish, Italian, and Greek.
